# 06: The Guardian speaks

**What to build:** When a Guardian questions a player, a dialogue box above the cards reveals ECO's Taunt and then the Question, letter by letter at a fixed rate. Enter or a click completes the text at once. The answer is typed inside the same box. The box shows the Question's Tier and what a miss costs (or that Ward cancels it).

**Rules change:** the Question's clock starts only when the Question is fully shown. The Expedition starts the deadline on demand, when the screen reports the text as shown, instead of when the Question is drawn.

**Taunts:** short Portuguese lines in the agreed tone (oppressive, no gore), chosen per Tier and Room kind (Combat, Elite, Exit) from the Question stream, so they follow the Seed.

**The statue reacts:** its eye glows in the answering player's colour while listening, it glitches on a miss, and it steps aside with its eye turned green when Cleared.

Sound comes in ticket 07; this ticket only exposes a hook per revealed character.
